### 1. Macro & Tape Analysis: Hyundai expected to outsell Ford in third quarter as Detroit

**Reporting Source:** CNBC Top News

DETROIT — Hyundai Motor is expected to outsell Ford Motor during the third quarter for the first time ever, according to a new forecast released Thursday by Cox Automotive.

The South Korean automaker is expected to report a 6.5% increase in year-over-year sales from July through September to 511,421 units. That compares with an expected 7.1% decline for Ford over the period to 504,172 new vehicles sold, according to Cox predictions.

The expected leaderboard change would make Hyundai the third best-selling automaker in the U.S. behind General Motors and Toyota Motor.
