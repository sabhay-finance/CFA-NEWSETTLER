### 1. Macro & Tape Analysis: White House still blocks some reporters despite judge's order

**Reporting Source:** CNBC Top News

A federal judge on Thursday ordered that White House access to journalists from CNN, MS NOW and Politico be immediately restored for up to two weeks, saying President Donald Trump 's media ban was likely unconstitutional.

Despite the ruling, some journalists from all three outlets were barred from entering the White House on Thursday morning.

MS NOW reporter Laura Barrón-López and an MS NOW producer had their press passes confiscated when they tried to enter, but a second MS NOW producer was allowed to enter. Politico said that at least one of their reporters was also blocked, and CNN reported that some of its staff was denied access, but at least one was allowed in.
