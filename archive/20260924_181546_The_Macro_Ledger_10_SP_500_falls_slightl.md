### 1. Macro & Tape Analysis: S&P 500 falls slightly as rising yields curb optimism over

**Reporting Source:** CNBC Top News

Stocks fell Thursday, as the move higher in yields restrained any optimism following a report of the U.S. and Iran potentially reaching a deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.

The S&P 500 dipped 0.1%, while the Nasdaq Composite lost 0.2%. The Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 162 points, or 0.3%.

Stocks came off their session lows after Reuters, citing sources, reported that U.S. and Iranian negotiators in New York are considering a deal that would bring a phased end to the conflict in the Middle East, whereby Iran would reopen the Strait of Hormuz and the U.S. would lift its economic blockade on Tehran.
